Hospitals pay for the software. Patients pay for extra care, or a programme pays for them. Watches sit on a monthly care plan, not a one-off gadget sale.

List prices below. They exclude tax. South Africa is billed in rand. Congo is billed in US dollars (private hospitals there already work that way). A 12-month term is the default.

## Hospital software, billed per site each month

| Plan | South Africa | Congo | What they get |
|------|-------------:|------:|----------------|
| Clinic | R 3,490 | $ 89 | One site. Outpatient admin, appointments, visits, patient chart. Up to 8 staff. |
| Hospital | R 9,990 | $ 249 | Everything in Clinic, plus beds, ward notes, collection counter, waiting screen, discharge invoice, occupancy numbers. Nurse job. Up to 25 staff. |
| Network | From R 19,990 | From $ 499 | Several sites. Quoted. Shared patient index and reporting as we switch that on. |

Extra staff seat: **R 249** or **$ 8** per month. No setup fee if they prepay the year.

## Patient app, billed per person each month

| Plan | South Africa | Congo | What they get |
|------|-------------:|------:|----------------|
| Free | R 0 | $ 0 | App, health records, book at their clinic, pickup codes. |
| Essential Care | R 149 | $ 6 | Reminders, longer device history, priority messages. |
| Complete Care | R 299 | $ 12 | Everything in Essential, plus video-visit credits and family sharing when that ships. |

Keep Free real. People should not pay only to see their file.

## Care packages with a watch, billed per person each month

Sold to a clinic, employer, medical scheme, or NGO for a group of patients. Hardware is included on a 12-month term.

| Package | South Africa | Congo | What they get |
|---------|-------------:|------:|----------------|
| Health Track | R 189 | $ 9 | E580 or E585 band, heart rate and oxygen in the app and on the clinic chart. |
| SafeCare | R 399 | $ 18 | Y6 Pro 4G watch, SOS and fall alerts, SMS to emergency contacts, clinic emergency board. SIM included. |

If the plan ends in the first year, the watch stays with RaphCare and can go to the next patient. After 12 months, say so in the contract if they keep it.

A private patient can buy the same packages at these prices. Essential Care without a watch stays R 149 or $ 6.

## Extra usage when the included bundle runs out

| Item | South Africa | Congo |
|------|-------------:|------:|
| Extra video visit | R 89 | $ 4 |
| Clinic AI add-on for summaries and chat, per site each month | R 1,490 | $ 39 |

SMS and emergency texts beyond the plan bundle are billed at cost. Do not bury an unlimited SMS promise in a cheap plan.

## How to sell this

1. **South Africa now:** invoice hospitals on Clinic or Hospital. Use Health Track or SafeCare when a site (or a scheme) will enrol a named group.
2. **Congo next:** same three hospital plans, quoted in dollars. Do not launch a consumer card checkout first. Sell programmes (a clinic, church, NGO, or employer paying for a list of patients). Patient mobile money can follow.
3. **Do not discount the watch to win the software deal.** The band is cheap. The month of monitoring is the product.

These figures are for partner discussion. Live card and mobile-money collection is not on yet. South Africa VAT is 15%. Congo tax is set on the local invoice when we have a local billing entity.
